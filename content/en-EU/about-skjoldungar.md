## Annual fee

The annual fee for 2026-2027 is **56.000kr**--.

### What is included in the annual fee?

The annual fee covers the weekly scout meeting schedule, patrol camping trips, scout scarfs (neckers), any badges the scout earns, some Skjöldungar identifying accessories, and subsidies for group camping trips.

### Sibling discount

A 10% sibling discount is applied automatically. Reykjavík City's "leisure card" (frístundakort) can still be used to pay the annual fee. Additionally, payments can be split.
